# Economic
- Due to the financial limitations of the CubeCats organization, we are limited to their already purchased hardware and budget for this project. 
- Because of this, we will focus on using open-source tooling and will not use proprietary software. 
# Professional
- This project must undergo a preliminary design review by professionals in the aerospace industry as part of the overarching project lifecycle. 
- Since we are operating within an organization with the University of Cincinnati, our actions and work will reflect the public opinion of the school and the CubeCats organization.
- The flight software shall follow established aerospace software engineering practices for requirements management, version control, testing, documentation, and verification.
# Legal
- Comply with all US regulations and procedures relating to export control and research security. 
- Comply with all US regulations and procedures for the utilization of radio frequencies.
# Security
- Payload data must be encrypted on downlinks to prevent foreign actors from accessing sensitive data. 
- Critical spacecraft commands shall require additional validation before execution to reduce the risk of accidental or malicious commands.
- The flight software shall isolate faults within individual subsystems to prevent a failure in one subsystem from unnecessarily compromising the operation of the OBC or other spacecraft subsystems.
