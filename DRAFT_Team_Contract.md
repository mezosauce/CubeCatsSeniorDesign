## Communication Ground Rules

Primary communication will occur in the dedicated project Discord server. Official documents, meeting minutes, and code will be centralized in GitHub and Google Drive. 

**Meeting Cadence:** Weekly meetings amongst just the senior design team will occur every Wednesday immediately following the senior design class. 

**Lab Sessions (optional, but strongly encouraged):** 

* Tuesdays: 6PM-8PM 
* Sundays: 12PM-1PM

**Advisor Meeting:** TBD 

**Response Expectations:** All members will acknowledge Discord pings within 1 day of receiving them, barring planned absences or illness. 

**Attendance Policy:** If a team member cannot attend a scheduled meeting or lab session, they must provide notice at least 6 hours in advance. 

## Decision-Making Process 

* **Consensus-Driven Approach:** All major design and architectural decisions go through a proposal and discussion, and are decided via team consensus. 
* **Fallback and/or Tie-Breaking:** If consensus cannot be reached, a majority vote (3-to-1) decides the path forward. In the event of a tie (2-to-2), the tie-breaking vote will be handed to the faculty advisor to make the final, executive decision.
* **Technical (Software) Changes:** Code will not be merged directly into the main branch. Every feature/issue will have the requirement of one approval before the Pull Request can be merged.

## Accountability & Conflict Resolution 

**Ownership:** Every member will take personal ownership of their work. If a blocker or external factor pops up that risks making a deadline or goal, the owner of that must notify the team immediately so tasks can properly be reallocated. 

**Conflict Resolution:** 

1. **Direct Discussion:** The conflicting members will directly address the issue to try to find an actionable compromise.
2. **Team Mediation:** If unresolved, the issue will be escalated to a team discussion to try to reach an internal resolution. Resolution should be focused on meeting project deliverables.
3. **Advisor:** If the team cannot resolve it, we will then escalate to the the faculty advisor to try to resolve the conflict.
4. **Course Coordinator:** If the team and advisor cannot resolve the issue, the final stage is bringing the conflict to the course coordinator for resolution.
